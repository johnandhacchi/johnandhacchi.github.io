The spanish Story

My spanish teacher is called Lillian Ninete.She is from Guatemala.Very kind,patient and good Christian.Sometimes I think she is a genius or a super woman because she always predict accurately about what I am thinking and can not find words to express myself in Spanish.That way she can teach me the exact words that I am looking for. There is time difference of 15 hours between Guate(my sensei would say short for Guatemala) and Japan.So I always erroneously say “good evening” instead of “good morning” To make it worse instead of saying buenos dias...I say buenas dias… and my wife Hacchi would always comment at the end of the lesson….”You said buenas dias...again !” so this makes me embarrassed always because I am old school and I believe that if I get it wrong at the foundation the whole construction is useless...
